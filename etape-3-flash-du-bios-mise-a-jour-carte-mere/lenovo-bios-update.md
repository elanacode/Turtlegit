# LENOVO (BIOS Update Guide)

This guide walks you through updating or rolling back the BIOS on **Lenovo** desktop and laptop computers (Legion, IdeaPad, ThinkPad, LOQ).

{% embed url="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AwOax1uWgYc" %}

***

{% hint style="danger" %}
#### Lenovo Safety Guidelines:
* Never disconnect power or force shut down your computer during the BIOS update process.
* On Lenovo laptops, plug in your original AC adapter and verify that battery charge exceeds 30%.
* In the Lenovo BIOS, if the **BIOS Back Flash** option exists, enable it if you plan to flash to an older version.
{% endhint %}

***

## Lenovo BIOS Update Steps

1. Visit the [Lenovo Support](https://support.lenovo.com/) website.
2. Auto-detect your serial number or type your model (e.g. *Legion 5 15ACH6H*).
3. Navigate to **Drivers & Software** ➜ **Manual Update** ➜ **BIOS / UEFI**.
4. Download the BIOS installation file (`.exe`).
5. Close all running software, right-click the file ➜ **Run as administrator**.
6. Follow on-screen instructions and accept the reboot prompt.
7. The PC reboots into a Lenovo firmware flash screen with a progress bar. Wait patiently until it finishes and boots back into Windows.
