## 1. Open Windows Security

Press `Win`, search for **Windows Security**, and open it.

{% endstep %}

{% step %}

## 2. Virus & Threat Protection

Go to **Virus & threat protection** ➔ **Manage settings**.

Turn OFF:
* **Real-time protection**
* **Cloud-delivered protection**
* **Automatic sample submission**
* **Tamper Protection**

{% endstep %}

{% step %}

## 3. Permanently Disable with dControl

To ensure Windows Defender does not re-enable itself:
1. Run **`dcontrol.exe`** (from our MEGA folder).
2. Click **Disable Windows Defender** until the icon turns orange/red.
